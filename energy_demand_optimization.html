Energy Demand Optimization

Project Overview

This project analyzes energy consumption across thousands of households using over 4.2 million data points. Our objective was to forecast peak demand and uncover patterns influencing energy usage, especially under rising temperatures.

Problem Statement

Energy providers struggle to balance supply and demand due to fluctuating usage patterns. We aimed to identify factors contributing to spikes and provide a tool to monitor demand trends interactively.

Dataset & Tools

  • Source: Smart Meter Data - South Carolina Domestic Energy Consumption Dataset
  • Size: 4.2M+ records from 5,000 homes
  • Tools: R, R Shiny, ggplot2, glmnet, caret

Modeling & Evaluation

We applied regularized regression models such as LASSO, Ridge, and ElasticNet using cross-validation to evaluate performance. ElasticNet provided the best trade-off between bias and variance with R² = 0.87. Models were validated on a test split and further evaluated on unseen future temperature conditions.

Real-World Impact

The insights derived from this project help utility providers understand future demand behavior and make informed infrastructure decisions. Consumers can also track their energy behavior over time to make more sustainable decisions.
